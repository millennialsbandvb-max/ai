Hi-hat (automatic, follows your tempo)

Stomp = kick (same detector as the first version). Snare = swing your leg out to the right. Once, with the phone in your pocket: tap "Set right", then do one clear swing to the right; the app remembers that direction (saved on this phone). After that, any swing that way faster than the slider's speed is a snare, and the stomp detector ignores the swing so you don't get a kick at the same time. After two stomps the app locks a clock to your tempo: the hi-hat runs on that clock, and with quantize on, each hit is nudged onto the nearest grid line.
