Where do you stomp?

Hi-hat

Every stomp is a kick. Keep a steady foot: the app measures your tempo and drops the snare on the backbeat and hi-hats in between on its own. Stop stomping for two beats and the groove stops. Lower the sensitivity slider if stomps are missed; raise it if walking sets it off.
