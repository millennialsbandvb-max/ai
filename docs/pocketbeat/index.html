Quantize stomps to

Hi-hat (automatic, follows your tempo)

Soft stomp = kick, hard stomp = snare. After two stomps the app locks a clock to your tempo: the hi-hat runs on that clock, and with quantize on, each stomp is nudged onto the nearest grid line so kicks and snares sit square with the hats. Stop for two beats and the clock stops. Orange line = kick threshold, yellow line = snare threshold.
