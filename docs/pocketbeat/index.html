Quantize stomps to

Hi-hat (automatic, follows your tempo)

Up / Down mode: a jolt while your foot is coming down (landing) is a kick, a jolt while it's going up (lift-off) is a snare. So a normal tap gives a kick, and a sharp upward flick of the foot gives a snare. The readout shows "dir" as the vertical speed at the moment of the jolt (negative = down). Other modes: the panel above shows every measure for your last stomp. Do a few taps, then a few lifts, and pick the measure whose numbers separate best; set its slider between the two. After two stomps the app locks a clock to your tempo: the hi-hat runs on that clock, and with quantize on, each stomp is nudged onto the nearest grid line so kicks and snares sit square with the hats. Stop for two beats and the clock stops. Orange line = kick threshold, yellow line = snare threshold.
