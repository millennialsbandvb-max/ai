Listens for the low thump of your foot (everything above 250 Hz is filtered out, so guitar and voice mostly pass through unnoticed). Stomp hard for a kick, lighter for a snare, two quick taps for a hi-hat. Use headphones or a Bluetooth speaker: if the drums play out of the phone's own speaker the mic will hear them and retrigger.
