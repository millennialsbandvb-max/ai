Hi-hat (automatic)

Heel / toe modes (board taped on the toes): tap "Set heel" and do 3 heel stomps, then "Set toe" and do 3 toe stomps. It tells them apart by which way your foot is rotating as it lands, and a toe also has to drop at least the "Toe lift" angle. The log shows each hit's lift angle: set the Toe lift slider between your heel numbers and your toe numbers. Stomp / swing mode (board taped on top of the shoe): a stomp above the orange line is a kick; swinging your foot in the learned direction faster than the swing speed is a snare. Teach the swing once: tap "Set swing" (or press the board's front button) and swing your foot 3 times the way you'll play it, pausing a moment after each; it learns the direction your foot starts moving and ignores the swing back. If it ever triggers on the swing back instead, tap "Flip swing direction". Tap / stomp modes: blue line = lightest tap that counts, yellow line = the stomp line. In every mode each hit sets the tempo; the hi-hat starts on your second hit and stops when you stop. The log shows each kick's impact (g) and each snare's swing speed (°/s), handy for setting the sliders. Everything here is stored on the board. Bluetooth and USB MIDI are always on.
