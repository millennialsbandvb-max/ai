Quantize

Hi-hat (automatic)

Stomp / swing mode (board taped on top of the shoe): a stomp above the orange line is a kick; swinging your foot in the learned direction faster than the swing speed is a snare. Teach the swing once: tap "Set swing" (or press the board's front button) and swing your foot 3 times the way you'll play it; the swing back doesn't count. Tap / stomp modes: blue line = lightest tap that counts, yellow line = the stomp line. In every mode each hit sets the tempo; the hi-hat starts on your second hit and stops when you stop. The log shows each kick's impact (g) and each snare's swing speed (°/s), handy for setting the sliders. Everything here is stored on the board. Bluetooth and USB MIDI are always on.
