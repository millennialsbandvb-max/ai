Swivel kit: lay the board flat on your foot. Foot straight = kick, swivelled out = snare, swivelled in = hi-hat (soft tap closed, hard tap open). Your hats set the tempo: play 6 in a row and the tempo snaps to them; kicks and snares just play on the grid. While you play hats yourself the auto hats stay quiet and come back two beats after you stop. Shake your foot hard for 3 beats to stop everything. "Center here" or the board's front button re-centres if it drifts. Set up for the right foot, USB-C toward the toes.

Looper

Simple version: everything you play is in the loop. Hats first (they set the tempo and land quantized), then kicks and snares on top. Tip your foot and hold it about a second = pause / resume (the board beeps). Front button = undo the last hit. Side button = erase the drum you're swivelled to. Hard shake for 3 beats = stop and clear everything. Same controls here on the page.

The impact meter at the top shows what each tap measures.

"Jolt only" takes the foot's movement (tilting, swivelling, sliding) out of the reading, so a tap measures how hard the foot actually hits, not how fast it was moving. Thresholds will read lower than before; re-check them on the meter.

MIDI notes

Defaults are General MIDI drums (36 kick, 38 snare, 42 closed hat, 46 open hat) on channel 10. Use the −/+ buttons to step one note at a time.
