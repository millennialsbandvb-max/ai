Hi-hat (automatic)

Default mode: stomp = kick, lift the foot high = snare (gyro), hi-hat automatic from your tempo. Light bounces of the leg (above the blue line, below the orange kick line) make no sound but set the tempo, so bounce to keep time and the hats follow. "Last lift" is how fast the leg rotated before landing. Everything here is stored on the board, so once it's tuned you can close this page and just play. Bluetooth MIDI is always on: pair the board in GarageBand (Settings › Advanced › Bluetooth MIDI) to use the phone's drum sounds with the phone locked.
