Each slide moves you up or down the range from wherever you are; Center puts you back in the middle. The board can only roughly measure how far you slid, so if notes jump by the wrong amount, set distance to 0 for exactly one note per slide.

Playing range

—

Swivel to where you want your lowest note and tap "Set low end here", then to the highest and tap "Set high end here". The notes spread evenly between them. Degrees per note and Notes in range still widen or narrow it around the same centre.

Feel

Tap harder for louder. A note holds while your foot stays where it was when you tapped; the green bar under the keys fills as you tip and turns red when the note will let go.

Sound

Bluetooth MIDI ("Footboard") and USB MIDI are always on.
